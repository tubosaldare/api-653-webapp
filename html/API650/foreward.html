Foreward

In the foreward to the API-650 standard it says the standard is based on the accumulated knowledge & experience of Purchasers & Manufacturers of welded oil storage tanks of various sizes & capacities for internal pressure ≯ 2½ PSI. This maximum pressure is often a question on the API-653 exam
